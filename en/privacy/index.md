# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## What data is collected
<p>The inquiry form collects your name, email address and organization name, and what you choose to explore. You can also add a short description, your role and a phone number, but these are optional. Source details are sent with the inquiry as well: the page you contacted us from, the site that referred you, and campaign parameters if the link included them.</p>

## Purpose of data processing
<p>The details are used only to read your inquiry, get back to you and check whether there is a basis for a conversation. They are not passed on to third parties and are not used for a mailing list.</p>

## Data retention and security
<p>Your inquiry is stored in PowOrg's spreadsheet and mailbox, in the company's Google Workspace account. An inquiry that did not lead to a conversation or to work is deleted after 24 months.</p>

## Your rights and data deletion
<p>You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>. Once the request has been carried out, we will confirm it by email.</p>

## Analytics and source attribution
<p>To count page visits, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. The tool does not use cookies and does not store details that identify you. The site has no tracking cookies.</p>

For questions or requests regarding privacy and personal data, please contact us at: <a href="mailto:{{ site.lead_fallback_email | default: 'contact@poworg.com' }}">{{ site.lead_fallback_email | default: 'contact@poworg.com' }}</a>.
