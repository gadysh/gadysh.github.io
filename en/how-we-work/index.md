<!-- 2. Two Starting Points (Spec §3.6, §6.3) -->
<section class="section section-alt" id="starting-points">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-header">
      <h2>Two starting points</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="entry-grid">
      <div class="entry-card">
        <h3>Have a specific process in mind?</h3>
        <p>Tell us what happens in it today, and we'll look at how AI can carry it out, assist with it or shorten it.</p>
        <p>In the first conversation, you describe the process as it happens today: who is involved, which systems you work in, and how many times it happens a day or a month. From there we check together whether AI can do part of the work, assist the people who do it or shorten it, and what it takes to connect it to your systems.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="entry-card">
        <h3>Want to understand where AI fits in your organization?</h3>
        <p>You don't need to come with a defined problem. We'll map the work in your organization together and identify where it makes sense to start.</p>
        <p>We start with a conversation about your work: which teams you have, what repeats itself, and where time goes on manual work in your systems. From there we choose together one or two processes worth examining first, based on the benefit and on what can be connected.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- 3. Work Track (Spec §3.6, §6.3) -->
<section class="section" id="work-track">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-header">
      <h2>Possible engagement track</h2>
    </div>

    <ol class="work-track-list">
      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">1</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Introduction</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">A conversation about your organization and its work.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>the person leading the initiative, and a general description of the work and the systems.</p>
      </li>

      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">2</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Finding opportunities and setting scope</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">Together we map the human work, and look at what can be handed over to agents and where AI can assist.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>a session with someone who knows the work closely, and examples from the work itself, such as documents, requests or recordings.</p>
      </li>

      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">3</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Solution architecture and technology choices</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">On our infrastructure or in your organization's cloud.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>details about the existing systems from your IT owner or vendor.</p>
      </li>

      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">4</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Development and system integration</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">We build the solution and connect it to your systems. Before it reaches users, we test it on cases from the actual work.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>access to the systems through an interface or through the vendor, and a test environment if there is one.</p>
      </li>

      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">5</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Rollout with users</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">Including training, and fitting it into the daily work and into contact with your customers.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>users who will work with the solution and give feedback early on.</p>
      </li>

      <li class="pillar-card work-track-item">
        <div class="work-track-header">
          <span class="step-num-badge m-0">6</span>
          <h3 class="m-0">Measurement, improvement and expansion</h3>
        </div>
        <p class="m-0">We check the results against what was defined at the start, and improve based on feedback. When it works, we decide together whether to expand to more processes.</p>
        <p class="step-needs"><span class="step-needs-label">What we need from you</span>a decision on what to measure, and a willingness to change rules based on what we learn.</p>
      </li>
    </ol>

    <p class="text-muted mt-6">Not every organization goes through every step, or in the same order. The scope and the form of engagement are set for each case.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- 4. Who Needs to Be Involved (Spec §3.6, §6.3) -->
<section class="section section-alt" id="team-involvement">

    </div>

    <div class="fit-grid fit-grid-auto">
      <div class="pillar-card">

        <h3>Systems vendor or IT owner</h3>
        <p>Opens access to the systems and approves how we connect to them.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <p class="text-muted mt-4 mb-0">In a small organization, this can be one person.</p>
  </div>
