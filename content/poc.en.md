# PowOrg's pilot project

## What is a pilot project?

A pilot is a working test run on a real process in your organization. It's not a slide deck and not a demo. It's a functioning setup that runs on your actual data and proves real, measurable value.

## What's included

### Mapping the process
- In-depth conversations with the people involved
- Mapping the current process and where it hurts most
- Clear success measures (time, accuracy, cost)
- Choosing the process with the strongest potential payoff

### Building it
- Building an AI assistant or processing engine tailored to your case
- Connecting it to your existing systems (CRM, ERP, documents)
- A simple, easy-to-use interface
- Testing on real data from your organization

### Testing and checking the results
- Running it against real test cases
- Comparing it to your current process (time, accuracy, cost)
- Collecting feedback from the people who'll actually use it
- Adjustments and improvements

### Handover and training
- Moving the pilot to a production or staging environment
- Full training for your team
- Technical and operational documentation
- Recommendations for what to do next

## Timeline

- **Weeks 1-2**: Mapping the process, defining success measures, collecting data
- **Weeks 3-5**: Building, connecting to your systems, initial testing
- **Weeks 6-7**: Testing with real users, adjustments, fine-tuning
- **Week 8**: Handover, training, wrap-up

**Total**: 6-8 weeks on average (depending on how complex the process is)

## What we need from you

- **Availability**: someone from your team who can check in regularly
- **Access to data**: real examples (anonymized if needed) of documents, calls, or other relevant data
- **Feedback**: availability of end users for testing and feedback
- **Infrastructure** (optional): if you want it running on your own servers, access to a development or staging environment

## How we measure success

### Numbers we track
- **Handling time**: how long a task takes before and after
- **Accuracy**: the error rate, or how often something needs a manual fix
- **Throughput**: how many tasks can be handled per day or week

### Things we listen for
- **User satisfaction**: is it actually easy to use?
- **Consistency**: are the answers and results reliable?
- **Flexibility**: how easy is it to add new cases later?

## What happens after the pilot?

If the pilot succeeds (and most do), there are two directions:

1. **Full rollout**: extending it to every relevant team in the organization
2. **Expanding to new processes**: using what we learned to identify the next process worth automating

We keep supporting, improving, and adding capabilities the whole way through.
